Tapsi · ride-hailing and delivery · 10M+ monthly users

Tap the city to call a car.

Searching the road graph from your tap. Nearest car by road, not by distance.

Every tap runs Dijkstra's algorithm from your pin across the road graph, and the first car it reaches gets the ride. The blue spread is the search. The straight-line distance is shown too, because it is always shorter and usually wrong.

At Tapsi I designed the Kotlin gRPC service behind every delivery estimate: an ML model first, falling back to road routing (OSRM), then a straight line. I owned it in production and moved five services onto it. With a data scientist, I took the team's first ML models live: estimate error fell from 24% to 19%.

Tapsi · ETA model server · 2026

54×

Every delivery estimate went through one model server. I found a serialisation bottleneck, removed it and batched the sub-model calls. Throughput per pod went from 70 to 3,800 requests a second; median latency at peak from 866 to 62 ms. Before it shipped I replayed 19,200 real requests. Every output was identical.

Research

Software Quality Research Lab, Sharif · BSc thesis · 2025 to 2026

Different models write better tests than one model with different personas.

TestCouncil asks Qwen, DeepSeek and Gemini to write unit tests for the same function, then merges them: 84.6% line and 92.5% branch coverage on 135 functions, against 49% line coverage for the best single model. Giving the models personas made no difference. Using different models did.

Research Assistant and thesis lead. Supervisor: Prof. Seyed-Hassan Mirian-Hosseinabadi.
