# about

I'm a software engineer focused on backend, distributed systems and ML infrastructure. For 3.5 years I worked full-time at Tapsi, a ride-hailing and delivery super app with 10M+ monthly active users, alongside my BSc at Sharif University of Technology. I was promoted to Senior Software Engineer in January 2024.

The work I enjoy most is making production systems measurably faster and safer to change: tracing a bottleneck, fixing it, and proving the outputs did not change before shipping. I owned services end to end, from design to code review and on-call.

I'm now doing an MSc in Software Systems Engineering at UCL and researching multi-agent LLM systems for automated software testing. Away from work, I'm a Codeforces Candidate Master.
